## The problem

Most fashion brands can tell you what a gifted campaign *cost*. Almost none can tell you what happened after the post went live — how many people actually visited, how many came back, how many bought. Gifting budgets get allocated on relationship and gut feel, not on a measurable return, because the data needed to measure it lives in three or four disconnected systems: your analytics platform, Shopify, your finance sheets, and whatever the influencer or agency reports back.

## What I built

A platform that tracks the full path from **post → visitor → return visit → purchase**, for every gifted campaign, in one place — alongside product performance, finance, and market data. Not a mockup: it runs on a real BigQuery warehouse with live SQL, the same kind of infrastructure a much larger analytics team would build.

## Why it's relevant to you specifically

- **Visitor-level attribution.** For every post, the platform shows first-touch visitors within 48 hours, how many return later, and how many convert — so "this post drove engagement" becomes a specific, measurable number instead of an impression.
- **True gifting ROI.** Cost, purchases, and revenue tied to each campaign precisely — so you can see which influencers are actually profitable and which aren't, sorted, ranked, filterable by market.
- **Scenario modeling.** "If we increase gifting budget by 20%, what's the projected return, and which influencers should get it?" — answered from your own historical performance, not a guess.
- **One place, not five tabs.** Product, finance, and influencer data joined together, so you're not reconciling spreadsheets across teams to answer a single question.
- **Built for exactly your context.** Multi-market from day one — a campaign performing well in one market and poorly in another is a different insight than a blended average, and the platform treats it that way.

## What this isn't

It's a prototype on a small, controlled dataset — not a finished product, and not yet running on your real data. What it demonstrates is the method: the data model, the attribution logic, and the questions it's built to answer. Pointing it at your actual GA4, Shopify, and finance data is a scoped, well-understood next step.
